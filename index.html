Chicken Dumplings

Planning a get-together? These crispy, juicy chicken potstickers make the perfect starter to get everyone reaching for more! Packed with tasty seasoned chicken and cabbaged, pan-fried until golden, and served with a delicious dipping sauce, they’re an easy crowd-pleaser for any occasion. Make a double batch to freeze a bunch for another time.

Ingredients

  • 4 chicken breasts(ground)
  • 2 tablespoons canola or vegetable oil, plus more for frying dumplings
  • 2 tablespoons minced ginger
  • 4 tablespoons minced garlic
  • 1 1/2 cups finely chopped cabbage
  • 1/3 cup chopped scallions
  • 4 teaspoons kosher salt
  • 3 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 1 teaspoon sesame oil
  • 32 to 40 round dumpling wrappers
  • water for cooking the dumplings
  • Chilli flakes (optional)
  • 2 tablespoons Worcestershire Sauce
  • Spring onion
  • 2 egg yolks

Dipping Sauce

  • 1/3 cup soy sauce
  • 1/3 cup rice vinegar
  • 1 clove garlic, minced
  • 1/2- inch piece of ginger, sliced
  • 1/2 teaspoon sesame oil
  • Siracha sauce
  • 3 tablespoons sesame seeds
  • 2 tablespoons Honey
  • 1/3 cup Worcestershire sauce

Instructions

  1. Add all the ingredients to a bowl
  2. Stir everything until well mixed and evenly seasoned

Prepare your working station

  1. Fill a small bowl with water for wrapping the dumplings
  2. Grab a baking sheet for the finished dumplings and a clean towel/kitchen cloth to cover the dumplings to prevent them from drying out
  3. Get a spoon for scooping the dumpling filling.

Make the dumlpings

  1. Place the wrapper on your left/less dominant hand, and wet the border of half of the wrapper facing away from you.
  2. Place about 1 tablespoon of filling into the center of the wrapper (you may use more or less depending on the size of your dumpling wrapper)
  3. Pinch together the wrapper on the right. Using your index fingers, create a pleat.
  4. Seal the pleat towards the right. Continue creating and sealing the pleats to the right until you reach the end of the dumpling. Use the photos above or this video as a reference.
  5. Continue pleating dumplings until you are out of filling or dumpling skins. If you notice that the dumplings are starting to dry out, cover them with a dry towel.

Cook the dumplings

  1. Heat a large nonstick pan with 1 1/2 tablespoons of oil over medium-high heat.
  2. Arrange the dumplings over the pan. I usually cook about 10 to 15 dumplings at a time. Pan fry the dumplings for 2 to 3 minutes, until the bottoms are golden.
  3. Next, hold the pan lid with one hand and pour about 1/4 cup of water into the pan with your other hand. You want just enough to cover the bottom of the pan. When the water comes in contact with the hot oil, there will be a lot of splattering, so use the lid of the pan as a shield. Cover the pan, reduce the heat slightly to medium, and cook for 6 to 7 minutes.
  4. Uncover the pan and let the dumplings cook for another minute or two, until the water is evaporated. Transfer the cooked dumplings to a plate.
  5. Repeat this entire cooking process if you want to cook the remaining dumplings. If you want to cook the dumplings later, freeze them.

Voila! Serve your dumplings

  1. Heat a non-stick pan with about 3 teaspoons of sesame oil over medium-high heat
  2. add the the soy sauce, worcestershire sauce, garlic, rice vinegar, ginger, and siracha sauce to the pan and let bring it to a boil
  3. Next, add the honey and sesame seeds and let it simmer(I like to prepare my sauce about 30 minutes before)
  4. Serve the potstickers with the dipping sauce and chopped spring onion
